# Research history

The public repository keeps the conclusions and content hashes needed to audit
decisions. Private prompts, hidden acceptance tests, repository snapshots, raw
transcripts, credentials, and superseded laboratory bodies are intentionally not
published.

## Accepted into V1

- Reuse-first, minimal-diff workflow and proof-level discipline.
- Outcome receipt with legal `unmeasured`, `pending`, and `N/D` states.
- Scenario-specific causal grading; file/line similarity demoted to diagnostics.
- One-writer integration and bounded escalation.
- S1 target behavior: OLD 0/3, NEW 3/3 (`722142eb…`).
- Installed causal-beta review receipt (`7496e9ab…`).

## Rejected

- External-provider credential doctrine: failed its mechanism test and remained
  outside the release (`5c57524d…`).
- Release claims based only on package/commit existence.
- Author self-classification of release doctrine.
- Similarity or Jaccard as acceptance, review, or release gates.

## Superseded

Multiple protocol and controller iterations hardened provenance, invalid-cell
semantics, timeouts, hidden-probe isolation, and human ratification. They are
research history, not product dependencies. V1 carries the resulting contracts
in a smaller implementation rather than publishing every experimental freeze.

## Admission rule

Future references enter the framework only when they supply a mechanism relevant
to an observed failure, a test, and a kill criterion. References do not alter a
frozen experiment mid-cycle.
